How we work

Five plain steps, the same for a two-week review or a six-month build. Every decision is written down, so nothing lives only in a call.

  1. Scope

    A short call about the product, the users and the constraints. You get a written scope and a quote, with what is in and what is out.

  2. Plan

    Architecture, milestones and the first week mapped out. Trade-offs are noted in the repo, next to the code they affect.

  3. Build

    Small, reviewed pull requests in your repository, deployed to a preview or UAT environment you can click through every week.

  4. Ship

    Production release through CI/CD, with a handover doc your team can run with and no steps that live only in my head.

  5. Support

    A bug-fix window after launch, then an optional retainer for new features, upgrades and upkeep.

Questions, answered

The things clients usually ask before the first call.

What are your rates?

There are no public rates. After a short scoping call you get a fixed quote for a defined scope, or a monthly figure for a retainer, in writing and before any work starts.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. Send a reasonable NDA before you share details and I will sign it. Client names stay private by default, which is why this site names none.

How do you handle the timezone difference?

Karāchi is UTC+5, which overlaps UK and EU working hours from your morning to mid afternoon. Calls go in that window, and daily written updates cover the rest.

Do you work outside Angular?

Yes. NestJS and PostgreSQL on the backend, Flutter for mobile companions, and React, Next.js or plain PHP for marketing and listing sites. Angular is where I am strongest, so that is where I add the most.

Are you open to full-time roles?

For the right remote team, yes. Use the contact form, choose Full-time role, and share the role and the team behind it.

How soon can you start?

Usually within one to two weeks of agreeing the scope, depending on current commitments. The availability badge at the top of this page is kept up to date.

Have something in mind?

A few lines about what you are building is enough to start. I reply to every message.
