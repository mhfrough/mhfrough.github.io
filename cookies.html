Cookie policyThere are none. Here is the detail.

Last updated 11 October 2026

In plain words

  • This site sets no cookies, first party or third party.
  • The notice at the bottom of the page only informs: there is nothing to consent to. Closing it saves one local storage flag so it stays closed.
  • The contact form keeps an unsent draft in your browser session storage. It never leaves your device.
  • Sites linked from here have their own cookie policies.

01No cookies

Cookies are small files a website asks your browser to store and send back on later visits. This site does not set any: no analytics cookies, no advertising cookies, no preference cookies and no session cookies.

You can check this yourself in your browser developer tools, under Application or Storage, while visiting any page.

02What the site does store

Two small things, both in your own browser storage, never in cookies, and never sent to me or anyone else:

  • When you close the cookie notice, a local storage entry named mh:cookie-notice with the value closed, so the notice does not come back. It holds nothing about you. Clear site data to remove it.
  • If you use the contact form, an unsent draft in session storage named mh.contact.draft while you type, so a reload does not lose your message. It contains only what you typed, is never sent to a server, and is deleted when you close the tab or once the email is drafted.

Downloading an icon or brand file, or using a copy button, stores nothing.

03Third parties

There are no analytics, ads or social widgets. The only outside content is the two Quorum videos on the home page and the Quorum case study, served from GitHub Pages when you press play. That is a plain file download, and GitHub handles it under its own privacy statement.

Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, Dribbble and live project sites take you to those services. They may set their own cookies once you are there, and their own cookie and privacy policies apply. I do not control them and am not responsible for them.

04Controlling cookies

There is nothing to opt out of here. Your browser settings let you block or delete cookies and site storage for every site, which also covers the services linked from this one.

05If this changes

If cookies or analytics are ever added, this page will be updated first, the privacy policy will name the provider, and you will be asked for consent wherever the law requires it. Questions are welcome at mhfrough@yahoo.com.
