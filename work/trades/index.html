Two front doors

SOS24 leads with its ten trades and a form. AK leads with one promise and a WhatsApp button. Same template underneath.

SOS24 is its original source built and served locally; its old domain now runs a different site. AK is the live site. Reviews and contact details are left out of every shot.

AK started as a copy of SOS24. What changed says more about the two businesses than about the code.

No server to look after

Small businesses don’t want a backend to maintain. The form writes a plain email and hands it to a serverless mail relay; AK’s button opens WhatsApp with nothing in between.

  1. 1Form or WhatsApp
  2. 2Plain-text message
  3. 3Mail relay or chat
  4. 4The owner’s phone

  • Enquiry by email

    Name and email are required; address, phone, postcode, trade and a message are optional. The message lands as one readable email, not a database row.

  • WhatsApp first

    For a one-engineer business the fastest reply is a chat. AK’s main button opens WhatsApp, loaded lazily so it costs nothing until it is needed.

  • Reviews

    One to five stars, a message of up to 475 characters, a name and an optional email. The home page slider reads them back live.

  • Prices before the call

    Emergency call-outs at £79 from 9 to 5, £119 from 5 to 10 and £179 from 10 at night to 6 in the morning; £49 when it can wait.

Decisions and what I’d change

Two small budgets, and owners who answer the phone themselves.

  1. 01

    Fork, don’t start again

    AK began as a copy of SOS24 and was finished in nine days. The same header, footer, form and page modules carried over; the work went into what was different about the business.

  2. 02

    Render where search matters

    SOS24’s ten trade pages are what people search for at 2 a.m., so they render on the server. AK’s main route in is a WhatsApp chat, so static files on Firebase were enough.

  3. 03

    Email, not a database

    An enquiry is a message for a person, so it arrives as a readable email through a serverless relay. Nothing to patch, back up or pay for while it sits idle.

  4. 04

    Config, not copies

    The fork carried SOS24’s trade list into AK’s form, locksmiths and all. Today the template would take a small config per business: trades, prices, contact routes.

  5. 05

    Reviews need a moderator

    Each review already carries a pending, approved or rejected status. What is missing is the screen where the owner decides, so nothing reaches the slider unread.

  6. 06

    Local SEO, properly

    Both sites set a title and AK adds Open Graph. A LocalBusiness block, a sitemap, the brand font actually loaded and real alt text would do more for a trades business than any redesign.

A small business that needs to be found?

Fast sites that turn a search into a call, without a server to look after.
