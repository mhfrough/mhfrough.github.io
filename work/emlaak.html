Two accounts, two rule sets

A quick account for small savers and a full one for everyone else. The app enforces each one’s limits at every step: investing, redeeming and switching funds.

  • Quick account

    Sahulat Sarmayakari

    For first-time investors who want to start small, with lighter KYC.

    • One form: CNIC details, address, income, zakat status and a nominee
    • Per-transaction, daily and yearly limits, checked on invest and redeem
    • Funds above Low risk ask for the risk profile first
    • NADRA CNIC front and back, nominee CNIC, CZ-50 and a utility bill
  • Regular account

    Sarmayakari

    No limits, and the full regulatory onboarding that comes with that.

    • Eight steps behind one progress bar, from contact check to declaration
    • A 5-digit OTP on the mobile number, and who it belongs to if not you
    • CRS and FATCA: a US question set and a W-9 upload when it applies
    • KYC questions on political exposure, refused accounts and offshore links

Decisions and what I’d change

Money and identity on one screen: every rule has to be visible, and every input has to be right the first time.

  1. 01

    Masks that know the bank

    The IBAN field is a form control and a validator in one directive. Pick a bank and its four-letter code is filled and locked; the rest must match the national format before the form is valid.

  2. 02

    Warn, don’t block

    An investor may choose a fund above their risk band. The app shows the gap and asks for a ticked disclaimer, so the choice is theirs and on record.

  3. 03

    Limits in one place

    The quick account’s per-transaction and daily limits are checked the same way on invest, redeem and convert, so no flow is the loophole.

  4. 04

    Hardening an older app

    In 2025 and 2026: a content security policy, sanitised alert messages against cross-site scripting, a 120-minute inactivity timeout, strict upload types and sizes, and account deletion.

  5. 05

    One risk profile, not two

    The questionnaire lives in the invest flow and again in the conversion flow. It should be one component both ask, so a scoring change lands once.

  6. 06

    Typed forms, then a new front door

    The KYC flow runs on template-driven forms in NgModules. Next is standalone components and typed reactive forms, then a public site that matches the app.
