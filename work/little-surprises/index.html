Ordering, the Pakistani way

Most people here order in a chat and pay on delivery. The bag turns into a WhatsApp message with every line spelled out, and checkout takes cash on delivery or a bank transfer with a photo of the receipt.

The problem and the approach

Gift shops here sell hampers someone else filled, and most ideas people save from Instagram are hard to order. The shop lets the buyer fill the basket.

  • Phone first

    Gifts are bought on phones, often late. A bottom tab bar, a raised Build action and a sticky total keep the next step under the thumb.

  • The basket sets the limit

    Each basket holds a set number of treats. The builder counts as you fill it and the server prices the same basket again before an order is placed.

  • Ready baskets to start from

    My wife makes baskets in the admin; buyers can buy one as it is or open it in the builder and swap what is inside.

  • Chat is a checkout

    Every bag can leave as a WhatsApp, Instagram or Messenger message, because that is where people here already shop.

What running it taught me

A shop is a different kind of client: the person filing the bug is across the table.

  1. 01

    Price on the server, always

    The builder shows a total as you go, but the API prices the basket again from its own catalogue before an order exists. The two can never disagree at the door.

  2. 02

    Say what is sourced

    Most items are bought after the order. Saying so on every product page cut the "where is my gift" messages more than any feature.

  3. 03

    The admin is the product too

    My wife runs the shop from the admin every day. Print slips, a today view and a shared inbox mattered more than another storefront page.

  4. 04

    No made-up parts

    Until the API answers, the builder shows nothing rather than sample baskets. A wrong price is worse than a short wait.

  5. 05

    Money in whole rupees

    Every amount is an integer, formatted only at the edge, so a basket of ten items can never be a rupee off.

  6. 06

    Marketing from the same repo

    Posts, stories, reels and gift tags are HTML rendered by scripts, so a price or a logo changes once and every piece follows.
