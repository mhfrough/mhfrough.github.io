Decisions and what I’d change

Many firms, each with staff and clients, on one codebase and two apps.

  1. 01

    Billing in its own app

    Sign-up, plans and subscriptions live in tenant management; client work lives in the workspace. Each deploys on its own path, and a payment problem never reaches a screen with client data on it.

  2. 02

    Scope in one place

    When a client signs in, one interceptor adds their account to every request. A new portal screen is scoped from its first line, without anyone remembering to do it.

  3. 03

    Templates before automations

    Organizers, requests, invoices, emails and tasks are templates first. A pipeline stage only points at them, so a firm changes its organizer once and every pipeline sends the new one.

  4. 04

    A grid for organizers

    Questions sit in rows that share their width, not in one long list, and each keeps its row, column and width. Firms lay out an organizer the way the paper one looked, and clients scroll far less.

  5. 05

    A code, not a token, in the URL

    The hand-off carries an access token in the address, where it lands in history and logs. Today I’d pass a short-lived one-time code and swap it for a session with a POST.

  6. 06

    One component library

    The workspace uses Bootstrap and the tenant app PrimeNG, so one product has two sets of buttons and dialogs. I’d pick one, move to standalone components and signals, and upgrade Angular with it.
