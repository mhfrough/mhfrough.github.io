Requests, not reservations

The resort is one property. A request never blocks anyone: it marks the slot as limited. The slot closes only when staff confirm, and confirming cancels every request that clashes with it.

The desk behind the gate

A small team runs the resort from the admin. Staff handle bookings and payments; admins also manage the gallery, packages and users.

  • Slot calendar

    A month at a glance, with Day, Night and Full Day status worked out in one query per month.

  • Bookings

    Approve, check in, check out, or cancel with a reason. Staff can add bookings by hand too.

  • Payment ledger

    Booking fees, deposits, refunds and deposit returns, in cash, bank transfer, cheque or online, with proof images.

  • Invoices

    An A4 invoice for every payment, drawn in the browser with jsPDF: preview it, then download.

  • Dashboard

    Pending and confirmed bookings, guests checked in today, revenue, and deposits paid, returned and held.

  • Roles

    Staff, admin and super admin. Only a super admin can see or create another super admin.

Decisions and what I’d change

A small business with one property needs fewer features and stricter rules.

  1. 01

    Requests first

    Guests ask, staff confirm. The resort talks to every group before a booking stands, so the system collects interest instead of turning people away, and still can’t double book.

  2. 02

    A ledger, not a gateway

    The resort is paid in cash and by bank transfer. Payments are signed entries, so a refund or a returned deposit is just a negative row, and the balance is one sum.

  3. 03

    Live on both sides

    Staff get a toast, a two-note chime and an unread count in the tab title. Guests watching the calendar see a slot close the moment it is confirmed. Staff connections are checked on the handshake.

  4. 04

    Images that cost less

    Uploads are resized to 1600px WebP on the server and cached for a year. The home page’s random gallery is cached for six hours, which keeps storage traffic down.

  5. 05

    One price function

    Weekday and weekend prices are worked out in the admin and again in the API. They should live in one place the site, admin and invoices all ask, so a rule change lands once.

  6. 06

    Check-in at the gate

    The API already models the paper check-in form: ID, vehicles, a towel and remote count, and a 20-area inspection. The next step is a tablet screen for it, so the deposit decision is on record.
